Signature treatment

The PRP Stem‑Cell Facial

A PRP facial uses a preparation made from a small sample of your own blood. We draw the sample, prepare the serum in a centrifuge, and apply it to your skin with micro-needling.

Medical director

Meet Dr. Linda McClintock

Dr. Linda is our medical director and president. She founded Age-Less Medicine in 2006 around quality-of-life medicine: helping people look and feel their very best.

For more than a decade she appeared as a health correspondent for Bay News 9. She oversees every part of your care and medically supervises our aestheticians. She is fluent in Spanish, and we welcome patients in English and Spanish.
