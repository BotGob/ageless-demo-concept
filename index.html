Medical director

Meet Dr. Linda McClintock

Dr. McClintock is the medical director and president of Age-Less Medicine, a practice she founded in 2006 around quality-of-life medicine: helping people look and feel their very best.

She appeared as a health team correspondent for Bay News 9 for over a decade. Her office specializes in age management, bio-identical hormone management, weight management, and facial rejuvenation with dermal fillers and muscle relaxants, as well as PRP stem-cell treatments for the face and hair.

Dr. McClintock oversees and medically supervises aestheticians who practice micro-needling, PRP applications, microdermabrasion, ultrasonic rejuvenation, and medical grade peels. She is fluent in Spanish. The practice holds values and ethics as a top priority, and patients are seen in a spa-like setting at One Urban Centre.

  • Advanced hands-on training in Botox, Restylane, Juvederm, and Perlane techniques from the institute of Aesthetics Medicine in Houston, and contouring with cosmetic fillers at Kapps Aesthetic Training in Toronto.
  • Member of the American Academy of Anti-Aging Medicine and the Hillsborough County Medical Association, with in-depth age management training at Cenegenics Medical Institute.
  • Earlier work includes service as medical director and later CEO of Health Ease, a medical plan owned by Tampa General Hospital, and as associate medical director for Humana Health Plan in Tampa Bay.
  • Before focusing on age and aesthetic medicine, she practiced as a pediatrician and served as a clinical instructor of family practice at the University of Texas Medical School in Houston, where she earned an Excellence in Teaching Award for 1987-1988.

The practice

Youth, wellness, and beauty from the inside out

Age-Less Medicine offers medically supervised weight management, skin rejuvenation, and age management through bio-identical hormone modulation. Beauty is not skin deep. Overall health has a direct impact on the aging process as well as outward appearance.

Hormone and age management

The focus is the science of youthful aging: maintaining health and energy for as long as possible. After a review of medical history, blood analysis, and other biological markers, an individualized program is created from hormonal modifications, nutrition, and exercise.

  • Hormonal labs
  • EKG
  • Body fat analysis
  • Nutrition consult

Couture Lifestyle Modification

A three-step, medically supervised weight program tailored to each person’s dietary and lifestyle needs, rather than a cookie-cutter diet. The first stage is an acute weight-loss phase. A transitional phase follows, then a maintenance phase with continued support.

  • Labs and EKG
  • Nutrition consult
  • Weekly visits
  • Vitamin injections

Signature treatment

How the PRP Stem-Cell Facial works

The practice describes this facial as using a preparation from your own blood, applied to the skin with a micro-needling technique. Scroll through the three steps published on the Age-Less Medicine services page.

Step 01

01

Draw

A small sample of blood is drawn. The practice describes this step as painless and notes that the facial uses a preparation made from your own cells.

02

Spin

The sample is placed in a special centrifuge that prepares the blood serum. That preparation is what the practice refers to as the PRP used for the facial.

03

Apply

The prepared serum is applied to the skin using the practice’s micro-needling technique. The same office also offers a PRP Stem-Cell Facelift, which adds an injectable step with hyaluronic acid filler before the remaining serum is micro-needled on the surface.
