Medical weight loss

We personally tailor each plan to your dietary and lifestyle needs. No more cookie-cutter diets.

Book a visit Hormone and age care

Our medically supervised weight program is a three-step plan. Dr. Linda designed it to give you knowledge, support, and a pace that can be maintained. Appetite suppressants, vitamins, and minerals may be included when they are medically appropriate. In some cases, hormone care may also be part of understanding what led to weight gain.

01

Acute phase

A carbohydrate and calorie-restricted diet begins this stage. We may add appetite suppressants, vitamins, and minerals when they are appropriate.

02

Transition

Medication is often decreased, some carbohydrates return, and we begin to encourage regular exercise.

03

Maintenance

The final stage is about support and guidance so you can stay with the habits that fit your life.
