Hormone and age management

Age management, for us, is the science of youthful aging: helping you maintain health and energy for as long as possible.

Book a visit Explore weight management

Your first visits with Dr. Linda begin with a careful look at your medical history, blood analysis, and other biological markers. From there she creates an individualized program that may include bio-identical hormone care, nutrition, and exercise.

We see this work as a partnership. The goal is a plan that fits your life, reviewed over time as your needs change.

Specialized testing

When it is helpful, we also offer specialized testing. Spectracell analysis looks at selected vitamins, minerals, antioxidants, and other micronutrients inside your white blood cells. ALCAT testing can identify foods that may be a problem for you. After your results return, you meet with Dr. Linda, and you may also meet with our nutritionist.

Is this visit right for you?

Who it is for

We welcome women and men who want a thoughtful look at how they age and how they feel. Dr. Linda creates an individualized program that may include bio-identical hormone care, nutrition, and exercise, based on your history, labs, and goals.

Your visit

What to expect

Your initial visit

  • Extensive hormonal labs
  • An EKG
  • Body fat analysis
  • A nutritional consult

Ongoing care

Follow-up visits are typically scheduled every three to four months. Labs are repeated as needed, or as Dr. Linda recommends.
