The Beauty Club

Dr. Linda’s Age-less Savings Account is a simple way to invest in your care each month and build Age-less Bucks toward our products and services.

Book a visit Visit the shop

Most of us plan for the years ahead. The Beauty Club is a way to plan for your skin, your health, and the treatments you already love. Each month you add to your Age-less Savings Account. Those funds, plus Age-less Bucks, can be used toward vitamins, nutrients, skin care, and services at Age-Less Medicine.

There are three levels. Retail discounts begin at 8 percent for Bronze and rise as your monthly amount increases.

Bronze

$99 a month

  • Service of your choice
  • 8% off retail products
  • Each quarter you invest $297
  • Plus $65 in Age-Less Bucks
  • $362 to spend on the service of your choice
  • 22% bonus back

Silver

$125 a month

  • Service of your choice
  • 10% off retail products
  • Each quarter you invest $375
  • Plus $110 in Age-Less Bucks
  • $485 to spend on the service of your choice
  • 29% bonus back

Gold

$200 a month

  • Service of your choice
  • 12% off retail products
  • Each quarter you invest $600
  • Plus $185 in Age-Less Bucks
  • $785 to spend on the service of your choice
  • 31% bonus back

Accounts are billed on the first of every month. You may cancel at any time with 30 days of written notice.
