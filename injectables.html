Facial rejuvenation

Injectables

Dr. Linda is internationally trained in facial rejuvenation with dermal fillers and muscle relaxants.

Book a visit Skin and facials

We use muscle relaxants and dermal fillers to soften lines and restore volume with a natural look in mind. Dr. Linda trained in Botox, Restylane, Juvederm, and Perlane techniques at the institute of Aesthetics Medicine in Houston, and in contouring with cosmetic fillers at Kapps Aesthetic Training in Toronto.

Muscle relaxants

Botox and Dysport are used on the face, around the eyes, the forehead, and the frown line. A 10 percent discount is applied when two or more treatment areas are treated. The amount needed can be higher for men.

Dermal fillers

We offer Restylane, Juvederm, Radiesse, and Restylane Lift, previously known as Perlane. These treatments are used to restore volume and fullness and to address moderate to severe facial wrinkles and folds.

Liquid facelift

Dr. Linda was among the first physicians in the area to lift the mid-face with Restylane Lift. This approach is also known as a Voluma-lift or liquid facelift, and may include Radiesse as well.

A note on comfort

Some filler visits use an anesthesia cream. For lip enhancement with Juvederm, a dental block can be performed so the visit is more comfortable.
