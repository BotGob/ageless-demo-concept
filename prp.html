The PRP Stem-Cell Facial

A signature treatment at Age-Less Medicine: a small blood sample is drawn, prepared in a centrifuge, and applied to the skin with micro-needling. The steps below follow the practice’s published description.

Book a visit See the three steps

The idea

Using a preparation from your own blood

Age-Less Medicine describes the PRP Stem-Cell Facial as a way of using a preparation from your own cells on the skin. On the public services page, the office explains that a small sample of blood is drawn and placed in a special centrifuge, which prepares the blood serum. That serum is then applied with the practice’s micro-needling technique.

Dr. McClintock oversees and medically supervises the aestheticians who practice micro-needling and PRP applications. The same office also lists PRP stem-cell treatments for hair, alongside this facial.

This page restates what the practice has already published. It is not medical advice, and it does not promise a particular result. A visit with the office is the place to ask whether the treatment is appropriate for you.

The process

Draw, spin, and apply

Three steps, taken from the Age-Less Medicine services page. On a large screen the diagram stays with you as you read.

Step 01

01

Draw

The office writes that it painlessly draws a small sample of blood. That sample is the starting point for the rest of the appointment. Because the facial is built from your own blood, the next steps stay close to that single preparation.

02

Spin

The sample is placed in a special centrifuge, which prepares the blood serum. Age-Less Medicine refers to the prepared portion as containing growth factors used in the facial. This page does not go beyond that public description.

03

Apply

The prepared serum is applied to the skin with the practice’s micro-needling technique. Micro-needling, as the office describes it, creates tiny channels in the skin and can be used on the face, neck, décolletage, and other areas of the body.

How the practice describes it

Published notes, not promises

On its services page, Age-Less Medicine describes the PRP Stem-Cell Facial as a treatment used to:

  • Minimize pore size
  • Reduce fine lines and wrinkles
  • Lift, tighten, and rejuvenate skin
  • Improve the appearance of stretch marks
  • Improve the appearance of acne scarring

Those lines come from the practice’s own public copy. They are not a guarantee of how any one person will respond, and this mockup does not add results claims of its own.

Also offered

Related treatments in the same room

PRP Stem-Cell Facelift

The office also publishes a facelift variation. In that version, PRP is separated and injected along with a syringe or two of hyaluronic acid filler, which the practice describes as a scaffolding. The remaining PRP serum is then applied with micro-needling on the surface of the skin.

PRP for hair, and micro-needling

Dr. McClintock’s published biography notes PRP stem-cell treatments for the face and hair. Micro-needling is also offered on its own. The practice describes it as a method used for the appearance of acne scars, hyperpigmentation, stretch marks, and fine lines and wrinkles.
