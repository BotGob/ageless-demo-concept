Skin care

The PRP Stem‑Cell Facial

A facial that uses your own blood's natural growth factors, paired with micro-needling, to refresh and renew your skin.

Book a visit See all skin treatments

What it is

What is a PRP facial?

PRP stands for platelet-rich plasma, the part of your blood that carries natural growth factors. At Age-Less Medicine we call this treatment our PRP Stem-Cell Facial. We draw a small sample of blood, place it in a special centrifuge to prepare the serum, and apply that serum to your skin with our micro-needling technique.

Because the facial uses a preparation from your own blood, it is designed for people who want a treatment built from their own cells rather than from an off-the-shelf injectable alone.

How it pairs with micro-needling

Micro-needling is how we deliver the prepared serum during the facial. We also offer micro-needling on its own. It can be used on the face, neck, chest, arms, hands, legs, abdomen, and back.

A related visit: the PRP Stem-Cell Facelift

If you want a more involved option, we also offer a PRP Stem-Cell Facelift. In that visit we place PRP with hyaluronic acid filler, then finish by micro-needling the remaining serum on the surface of the skin.

Is this visit right for you?

Who it is for

We offer the PRP Stem-Cell Facial to women and men, and for all skin types and tones. Many guests come to us when they want a physician-supervised facial that may help the look of pores, fine lines, wrinkles, stretch marks, or acne scarring. We also offer PRP stem-cell treatments for hair.

Your visit

What to expect

Your time with us follows three clear steps.

01

Draw

We begin by drawing a small sample of blood. This is the starting point for the rest of your visit.

02

Spin

We place the sample in a centrifuge that prepares the blood serum we will use on your skin.

03

Apply

We apply the prepared serum with our micro-needling technique, which creates tiny channels in the skin.
