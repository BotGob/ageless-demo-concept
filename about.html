Our physician

Dr. Linda McClintock

Medical director and president of Age-Less Medicine, and a familiar health voice in Tampa Bay.

Book a visit Visit the office

Dr. Linda founded Age-Less Medicine in 2006. She leads the practice by hand and supervises every part of the care we deliver, from hormone and weight programs to injectables and skin treatments.

For more than a decade she appeared as a health correspondent for Bay News 9. She is a member of the American Academy of Anti-Aging Medicine and the Hillsborough County Medical Association, and she completed in-depth age management training at Cenegenics Medical Institute.

She trained in Botox, Restylane, Juvederm, and Perlane techniques at the institute of Aesthetics Medicine in Houston, and in contouring with cosmetic fillers at Kapps Aesthetic Training in Toronto. She is fluent in Spanish.

A longer view of her work

  • From 1994 to 1998 she served as medical director of Health Ease, a medical plan owned by Tampa General Hospital, and later as its chief executive officer.
  • From 1993 to 1994 she was associate medical director for Humana Health Plan, which at the time was Tampa Bay’s largest health plan.
  • From 1986 to 1993 she practiced as a pediatrician and served as a clinical instructor of family practice at the University of Texas Medical School in Houston, where she earned an Excellence in Teaching Award for 1987-1988.
  • She has been active in Tampa civic life, including founding Friends of the Tampa Police Department.
